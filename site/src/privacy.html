Privacy policy

Effective {{effectiveDate}}. This policy covers the Weekwell app and service, provided by {{company}} (“we”, “us”).

The short version

  • Weekwell plans your meals and builds a grocery list. To do that it needs your store, budget, cooking time, household size, and the foods you want to leave out.
  • If you use the app without an account, that information stays on your phone.
  • If you sign in, we keep it on our servers so it’s on all your devices. We store a keyed hash of your email address, not the address itself.
  • We don’t sell your personal information, share it for targeted advertising, or track you across other apps and websites.
  • You can delete your account and everything in it from the app at any time.

We don’t collect your precise location, contacts, photos, microphone, or payment card details. Apple handles payments; we never see your card.

How we use information

We use it to provide the service: planning meals, building your grocery list, estimating prices, syncing your devices, managing your subscription, answering support requests, and keeping the service secure and working. We don’t use your information for advertising, and we don’t build profiles to make decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects on you.

Who we share it with

We share personal information only with service providers that process it for us under contract, and only as needed:

  • Resend: delivers your sign-in code email (your email address and the code).
  • RevenueCat: manages subscriptions (your account id and purchase and subscription records from the App Store).
  • Apple: processes payments and subscriptions under Apple’s own privacy policy.
  • {{hostingProvider}}: hosts our servers and database.

We may also disclose information when the law requires it, to protect the safety or rights of people or the service, or as part of a merger or sale of the business (in which case this policy continues to apply to your information).

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral (targeted) advertising. We have not done so in the past 12 months.

How long we keep it

  • Account data (preferences, foods you leave out, plans, lists, subscription status): until you delete your account.
  • Sign-in codes: 10 minutes. Sessions: up to 30 days, or until you sign out.
  • Security logs: up to 30 days.
  • Backups: deleted data leaves our backups within 35 days.
  • Data on your device: until you delete it or remove the app.

Your choices and rights

Wherever you live in the United States, you can:

  • Delete your account and data in the app: Preferences → Delete my account. Without an account, choose Delete my data.
  • Access or get a copy of your data: email us and we’ll send it in a portable format.
  • Correct your data by editing your preferences in the app, or ask us.
  • Opt out of sale, targeted advertising, and profiling. We don’t do any of these, so there’s nothing to opt out of, but you can still ask.
  • Limit the use of sensitive information. We use foods you leave out only to plan your meals.

Residents of California, Colorado, Connecticut, Delaware, Iowa, Indiana, Kentucky, Maryland, Minnesota, Montana, Nebraska, New Hampshire, New Jersey, Oregon, Rhode Island, Tennessee, Texas, Utah, Virginia, and other states with privacy laws have these rights under their state law. If we deny your request, you can appeal by replying to our decision; if we deny the appeal, you can contact your state attorney general.

To make a request, email {{privacyEmail}} from the address you sign in with, or write to {{mailingAddress}}. We verify requests by confirming control of that email address. You may use an authorized agent, and we may ask the agent for proof of authorization. We respond within 45 days, and we won’t treat you differently for exercising your rights.

Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track

We don’t sell or share personal information or track you across sites, so a Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track signal doesn’t change what we do. We treat a GPC signal as a valid opt-out request.

Children

Weekwell is not directed to children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we’ll delete it.

Security

We use encryption in transit, store only hashes of email addresses, session tokens, and sign-in codes, limit and log access, and scope every record to its owner. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects your personal information, we’ll notify you as the law requires.

Where we process data

We process data in the United States.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes, we’ll update the effective date and tell you in the app before the change takes effect.
