What’s in it

Swap any meal

Not feeling Tuesday? Choose from three alternatives, see the time and price difference, and undo if you change your mind.

Built around your budget

Each week is planned to your weekly target. If the estimate comes in over, Weekwell shows where the money goes and how to save.

Foods you leave out

Switch off dairy, gluten, nuts or anything else, and those recipes never show up. Always check package labels too.

Cooking mode

Every step on one screen, the current one large, with timers that keep running while you prep.

Your plan stays yours
