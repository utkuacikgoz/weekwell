Questions

Which stores does Weekwell work with?

Trader Joe’s and Walmart today. Prices are estimates for your store and can differ at the register. Weekwell isn’t affiliated with either.

Are the prices exact?

No. They’re estimates, labelled as such, and each dinner shows its share of the week’s estimated total so you can see where the money goes.

I have a food allergy. Can I rely on it?

Leaving a food out removes every recipe that uses it, but products and factories change, so always read package labels. Weekwell isn’t medical advice.

Do I need an account?

No. Your plan, preferences and the foods you leave out stay on your phone. Subscriptions go through your Apple ID.

How do I cancel?

On your iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Weekwell → Cancel Subscription. You keep access until the end of the period.
