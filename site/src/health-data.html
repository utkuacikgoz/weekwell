Consumer health data privacy policy

Effective {{effectiveDate}}. This policy is part of our privacy policy. It explains how {{company}} handles information that some state laws, such as Washington’s My Health My Data Act and Nevada’s consumer health data law, may treat as consumer health data.

What we collect

When you tell Weekwell about foods you leave out (for example an allergy to peanuts or a dairy-free diet), that choice may reveal something about your health. That, together with your meal goal (for example “high protein”), is the only health-related information we collect. We don’t collect diagnoses, medications, weight, or body measurements.

Why we collect it and how we use it

Only to plan meals that leave those foods out, and to warn you when your choices leave too few meals to plan a week. We never use it for advertising, and it is never included in analytics or logs.

Where it comes from

Only from you, when you enter it in the app.

Who we share it with

It stays on your device. We don’t receive it, sell it, or share it with anyone.We don’t sell consumer health data, and we don’t share it with anyone except our hosting provider ({{hostingProvider}}), which stores it for us when you sign in. If you use the app without an account, it stays on your device.

Your rights

  • Confirm and access: ask whether we have your consumer health data and get a copy, including a list of anyone we shared it with.
  • Delete: remove the foods in the app, or delete everything (Preferences → Delete my data).
  • Delete: delete it at any time by removing the foods in the app, or delete your whole account (Preferences → Delete my account). We also delete it from backups within 35 days.
  • Withdraw consent: remove the foods in the app. We then stop using them.

To make a request, email {{privacyEmail}} or write to {{mailingAddress}}. We respond within 45 days. If we deny your request, you can appeal by replying to our decision; if we deny the appeal, you can contact the Washington State Attorney General or your own state’s attorney general.

Consent

We collect foods you leave out only when you choose to enter them, and only to provide the meal plan you asked for.
