# Food photography checklist (D-038)

Generated by `npm run photos`: 0 of 22 recipes have an approved photo. The app switches from the illustrated plates to photos only when all 22 are present, so the two styles are never mixed.

## Spec

- One master per recipe: 1600×1200 or larger, 4:3, JPEG or PNG, under 500 KB, saved as `apps/mobile/assets/meals/<recipe id>.jpg`.
- The app crops the master to a 16:9 hero and a 1:1 thumbnail. Keep the dish centred.
- Style (append to every prompt): Editorial food photograph, 3/4 view from about 45 degrees, soft window light from the upper left, matte stoneware on warm natural linen, shallow depth of field focused on the main protein, a portion for one person, no hands, no text, no brand packaging, warm neutral colour grade, 4:3 frame with the dish centred and 15% breathing room on every side.
- Show only ingredients that are in the recipe. Garnish only with recipe ingredients.
- One dish per photo, in the vessel named in the brief.
- Same light, surface and grade across all 22. Reshoot any image that breaks the set.
- Generated stills are labelled "Illustrative photo" in the About sheet and store listing. A person checks every image against the ingredient list before it ships.

Full prompts for an image tool: `apps/mobile/assets/meals/prompts.json`.
