# Weekwell privacy policy (DRAFT, not legal advice, needs legal review)

_Last updated: draft of 2026-09-24_

Weekwell helps you plan five dinners and work lunches and builds a grocery list. This draft describes what the app does today.

## What we collect

**If you use Weekwell without an account** (the default pilot build), your plan, preferences, the foods you choose to leave out, and your grocery checks stay on your device. We don't receive them. Usage events used to improve the app are kept on your device and are not sent anywhere.

**If you sign in** (builds connected to the Weekwell service):

- **Email address:** used only to send you a sign-in code. We store a keyed hash of it to recognize your account, not the address itself.
- **Your plan and preferences:** store, budget, goal, cooking time, household size, foods you leave out, meal plans, and grocery checks, so they're available on your devices.
- **Subscription status:** trial or plan and its dates.

We don't collect your location, contacts, photos, or payment card details (payments are handled by Apple).

## Foods you leave out

Allergies and foods you leave out can be health-related, so we treat them with extra care. They're used only to plan meals and never included in analytics or logs.

## How we use data

To plan meals, build your grocery list, estimate prices, keep your subscription status, and keep the service secure. We don't sell your data or use it for advertising, and we don't track you across other apps or websites.

## Keeping and deleting data

You can delete everything at any time in the app (Preferences → Delete my account, or Delete my data without an account). Deletion removes your account and all your plans, preferences, and subscription records from our service. [Retention for backups and security logs: to be set by the product owner.]

## Prices and nutrition

Prices are estimates and can change in store. Protein values are estimates from typical ingredient values and aren't medical or dietary advice. Weekwell isn't affiliated with any retailer.
